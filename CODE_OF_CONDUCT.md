# Code of conduct

MorAssistant contributors are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and safe.

- Treat other people with patience and professional respect.
- Critique ideas and code without personal attacks.
- Do not harass, discriminate against, threaten, or expose private information about others.
- Respect the confidentiality of security reports and private CAD data.
- Accept maintainer decisions made to protect the community and project.

Unacceptable behavior may be reported privately through the repository's security-advisory channel. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when necessary to enforce these expectations.
